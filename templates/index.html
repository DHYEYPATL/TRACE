Identity-aware, not just class-aware

"Bottle" is a class, not an identity. AssetWatch uses deep features + colour + keypoints and declines ambiguous matches. The gallery auto-learns new angles over time.

Camera health gates alerts

A disconnected or stale camera suspends missing-item decisions. Short gaps = CHECKING; confirmed absence = MISSING.
